DAY 4 · WEEKLY SURRENDER DEVOTIONAL

Walking in Purpose

Ephesians 2:10 · Taking the next faithful step

Scripture Focus
Ephesians 2:10
Open your Bible and read the full passage before beginning.
This day’s reflection

Purpose is not always revealed in one dramatic moment. Often, it becomes clearer through ordinary acts of obedience, service, learning, and persistence. God can use the gifts and experiences you already have.

You do not need to know the entire path to begin. Consider the people you can serve, the strengths you can develop, and the work in front of you right now. Small steps can create meaningful impact over time.

Surrender in Action

Identify one gift you can use today to encourage or help someone. Make a simple plan and follow through.

Prayer for the Week

Lord, help me recognize the opportunities You have placed before me. Lead me to act with humility, courage, and love. May my life be used for good. Amen.
