A Glimpse Inside the Journey

Preview the four phases and track your introductory lesson reflections in this browser.

Your Learning Path

0 of 4 introductory lessons completed

Lesson 1: Awareness

Ask yourself: What am I holding onto that is keeping me from moving forward? Read Psalm 139:23–24 and name one pattern you want God to help you examine.

Lesson 2: Release

Read 1 Peter 5:7. Write down one burden you can bring to God in prayer this week.

Lesson 3: Renew

Reflect on Romans 12:2. Identify one thought pattern you want to challenge with truth.

Lesson 4: Build & Maintain

Read Ephesians 2:10. Choose one small habit that helps you live more intentionally in your purpose.
