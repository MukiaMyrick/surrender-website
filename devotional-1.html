WEEK 1 · WEEKLY SURRENDER DEVOTIONAL

The Power of Surrender

Proverbs 3:5–6 · Trusting God when you do not have every answer

Scripture Focus
Proverbs 3:5–6
Open your Bible and read the full passage before beginning.
This week’s reflection

Surrender is often misunderstood. We sometimes think it means defeat, weakness, or giving up on what matters to us. But biblical surrender is not a loss of purpose. It is a decision to trust God with our lives, including the parts we cannot plan or control.

Maybe you have been trying to carry a burden that was never meant to rest entirely on your shoulders. Maybe you are holding on because letting go feels uncertain. Faith does not require knowing the outcome before taking a step. It invites us to acknowledge God in the middle of the unknown.

Surrender in Action

This week, identify one area where you have been demanding certainty. Write it down. Pray over it, then choose one action that reflects trust rather than fear.

Prayer for the Week

Heavenly Father, teach me to trust You beyond my understanding. Help me release the need to control every outcome and take faithful steps today. Guide my heart and direct my path. Amen.
