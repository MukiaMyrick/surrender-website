DAY 3 · DAILY SURRENDER DEVOTIONAL

Becoming Who God Created You to Be

2 Corinthians 5:17 · Living beyond your old labels

Scripture Focus
2 Corinthians 5:17
Open your Bible and read the full passage before beginning.
This day’s reflection

Our past experiences can shape how we see ourselves. We may begin to believe that our mistakes, disappointments, or struggles are the whole story. Scripture points us toward renewal and the possibility of a new way of living in Christ.

Becoming is not about pretending yesterday never happened. It is about allowing God to reshape how we think, choose, and respond today. Growth can be gradual. Every faithful step matters.

Surrender in Action

Write down one label you have placed on yourself that no longer reflects who you are becoming. Replace it with a truthful, hopeful statement and one action you can practice.

Prayer for the Week

Father, renew my mind and help me see myself through the lens of Your love. Give me courage to grow and grace for the process. Amen.
