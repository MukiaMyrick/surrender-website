SURRENDER WEBSITE — PREVIEW PACKAGE

Open index.html in a browser to view the website. All navigation links use separate HTML pages.

To publish: Upload the entire surrender-website folder to a static host such as Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or GitHub Pages. Or adapt its layout/content in Wix.

IMPORTANT: Email signup and contact forms are preview-only; they do NOT submit or collect contact details. Connect a mailing list/contact form service before accepting signups. Student course tracking and journals use local browser storage only, not secure student accounts. Payments, course video hosting, and live community features are not connected.

The site uses original uploaded photos for accuracy, not AI-generated likenesses. The hero references assets/founder-hero.jpg. Google Fonts are loaded online if available.

Do not publish without checking text, rights, privacy policy and contact details.

SHOP UPDATE: shop.html is the new separate storefront preview. Product formats, pricing, payment processing, shipping, and digital delivery are NOT yet connected. Replace placeholders with verified product details before launching.
