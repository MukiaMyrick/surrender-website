WEEK 2 · WEEKLY SURRENDER DEVOTIONAL

Letting Go of What You Cannot Control

1 Peter 5:7 · Releasing worry and making room for peace

Scripture Focus
1 Peter 5:7
Open your Bible and read the full passage before beginning.
This week’s reflection

Worry can make us feel responsible for every outcome, every relationship, and every unanswered question. Yet God invites us to bring our cares to Him rather than keep carrying them alone.

There is a difference between being responsible for your actions and trying to manage everything around you. Surrender allows you to do what is yours to do while leaving what is beyond your reach in God’s hands.

Surrender in Action

Draw two columns in your journal: “What I can influence” and “What I must release.” Choose one healthy action from the first column, and pray over the second.

Prayer for the Week

God, You know what is heavy on my heart. Help me care deeply without carrying what I cannot control. Give me wisdom for my part and peace about the rest. Amen.
