# SURRENDER Shop — Product & Fulfillment Setup

## Catalog to create in Wix Stores
1. Surrender Prayer Journal — Digital PDF (separate **Digital file** product)
2. Surrender Prayer Journal — Printed book (separate **Physical** product)
3. The SURRENDER Devotional — Digital PDF (separate **Digital file** product)
4. The SURRENDER Devotional — Printed book (separate **Physical** product)

You can link the digital and printed listings from each title's landing page. **No price has been invented, and no checkout is active in the prototype.**

## Digital fulfillment (Wix Stores)
1. Finalize an accessible, readable PDF and cover preview. Do not upload unfinished manuscripts.
2. In your Wix dashboard, choose Products → New Product → Digital file, add product description, cover art, final downloadable PDF, price and tax category.
3. Connect a supported payment method, configure checkout and test a complete purchase.
4. Once Wix receives payment it emails a download link and includes it on the thank-you page. Check spam and mobile download flows in testing.
5. Display clear digital product terms, permitted personal use, and refund policy; configure applicable tax collection.

## Printed book fulfillment (Lulu Direct + Wix)
1. Finalize the book interior and full print cover PDF separately according to Lulu's specific trim size, page count, bleed, binding and spine templates.
2. Make a Lulu book project. Order and inspect a printed proof *before* making the listing live.
3. Add a Physical product to Wix. Connect your Wix store to the Lulu Direct integration and map each physical book to its Lulu project.
4. Verify Lulu shipping mapping, shipping rates in Wix, domestic/international destinations, printing cost, taxes, payment funding, tracking, returns and damaged-item procedures.
5. Place a real test order. On live orders, customers pay your store and **your business pays Lulu separately for printing and shipping**. Maintain a suitable payment method and buffer for fulfillment costs.
6. Set retail price after subtracting print costs, shipping subsidy, payment fees, sales tax impact and margin goals. Final prices require actual page counts/specifications and shipping regions.

## Store policies and readiness
- Legal business/contact information, privacy policy, terms, consent-based marketing signup, delivery timelines, refunds/returns, and shipping coverage
- Product images use actual final cover designs, not illustrative concept art
- Confirm ownership/permissions for every scripture translation quoted and any third-party illustrations
- If you bundle physical + digital editions, test mixed carts and associated shipping and download fulfillment
- Add other faith-based merchandise later with inventory or a separate POD provider as appropriate
- Monday 7:00 AM ET devotionals subscription is a separate email marketing workflow; it is **not** activated by this static prototype
